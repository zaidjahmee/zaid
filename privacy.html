Ledger ("the app") is an internal business tool used by [FILL IN YOUR BUSINESS NAME] to track daily inventory, sales, cash reconciliation, and employee shifts across our store locations. This policy explains what data the app stores and how it's used.

What data is collected

The app stores:

  • Daily inventory counts and sales figures per store
  • Cash/card amounts collected per day
  • Employee shift records: employee name, shift start/end time, pay amount, and notes entered by whoever fills in the shift

The app does not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any data from your phone beyond what is manually entered into the app by staff.

How data is stored

Data is stored in a cloud database (Supabase) so that authorized staff and store locations can see up-to-date figures, and is cached locally on-device so the app keeps working without an internet connection. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside operating this business.

Who can access data

Data is accessible only to devices running this app that have been given access by [FILL IN YOUR BUSINESS NAME]. It is not publicly accessible.
